How the funnel works
From ad to tripping.

      
Every visitor is tracked from the ad to the agent's Messenger, so your ads learn who actually books a tripping.

    

      
Step 1Facebook ad
Sends people to the site or to an agent's own link.

      
Step 2Showcase site
Every PHirst project: video, photos, floor plans, route map and the payment calculator.

      
Step 3Booking form
Name, mobile, project, and preferred tripping day and time.

      
Step 4Alerts fire
Pixel "Lead" to Meta, Telegram to the agent, and a row in the Google Sheet.

      
Step 5Messenger
Opens on the Facebook page the client came from, ready to confirm the tripping.
