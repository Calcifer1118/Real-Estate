Questions
Good to know.

    

      
What if PHirst changes prices?
Your care plan covers it. We update prices and the calculator every quarter when PHirst releases a new list, and inventory every week.

      
Can agents edit their own page?
No, and that's on purpose: every agent page shares one template, so it always looks right. Send us changes and they apply to all agents at once.

      
What if we stop the care plan?
After the 3-month minimum, give 30 days' notice. The site stays up, but prices and information are no longer updated and go out of date. You keep the domain, the logo and every lead.

      
Do we still need to run ads?
Yes. The site turns visitors into booked trippings; your ads bring the visitors. The tracking shows which ads actually lead to bookings, so you can put your budget where it works.

      
We sell other PHirst projects. Can they be added?
Yes, for free, anywhere in the Philippines. Send us its photos and videos, plus the price list and computation sheet, and we'll build its page with the calculator and map.

      
Who do clients pay?
PHirst directly, as always. The site never collects payments from buyers.
